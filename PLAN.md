# Ellie & Jaiden's Wedding Website — Project Plan

This file is just for you to see the whole scope at a glance. It's not
part of the website itself — feel free to edit or delete it anytime.

**Style reference:** sage green palette, flowing script headlines
("Great Vibes" font), envelope click-to-open interaction.
Inspiration screenshots are saved in `images/inspiration/`.

**Files:**
- `index.html` — the page content/structure
- `style.css` — all the colors, fonts, layout, and animations
- `script.js` — the click interactions (opening the envelope, etc.)
- `images/photos/` — your real photos
- `images/inspiration/` — the reference screenshots you shared

---

## Milestones

### ✅ Milestone 1 — Envelope screen
Closed sage-green envelope with "You've got mail..." script headline,
"Open Me" embossed on the flap, and a wax seal. Clicking it opens the
flap. **Done and confirmed working.**

### ⬜ Milestone 2 — Save the date reveal
What happens right after the envelope opens:
- Background changes to a full-screen photo of you two
- A smaller envelope slides in with a "Save the Date" paper peeking out,
  showing the date (May 30th, 2027)
- Two polaroid-style photos appear scattered near it
- Uses real photos from `images/photos/`

### ⬜ Milestone 3 — Photo filmstrip section
A second photo section below the save-the-date reveal — 3 photos side
by side in a simple filmstrip/frame layout.

### ⬜ Milestone 4 — RSVP info form
A section asking guests for:
- Full name(s) of everyone in their household
- Preferred name for mailing (e.g. "The Smith Family")
- Mailing address
- A submit button
(Styling and layout only for now — see Milestone 6 for making it
actually send you the data.)

### ⬜ Milestone 5 — Footer
Sage-green footer with "With Love, Ellie & Jaiden" in script, and a
"Back to envelope" link that scrolls back to the top and resets the
envelope so it can be opened again.

### ⬜ Milestone 6 — Connect the form for real
Right now the submit button won't actually go anywhere. This step wires
it up so submissions really reach you (decision on how — e.g. Formspree
or a Google Form — still to be made).

### ⬜ Milestone 7 — Hosting
Get the finished site live at a real, shareable web address.
